Neural Correlates of Analysis and Synthesis Across Domains

Neural Correlates of Analysis and Synthesis Across Domains #

Status and boundaries #

This page is a cautious bridge between neuroscience literature and BWB's project hypotheses. It does not locate “analysis,” “synthesis,” or a typological aspect in one brain region, and it does not infer type from neuroimaging. The fMRI BOLD signal indirectly reflects blood-flow changes; it is not a photograph of thought, ability, or energy expenditure.

The working sequence is: task → distributed network of operations → behavioural and, when appropriate, physiological measures. Reverse inference from regional activation to a particular mental process remains uncertain.

Working definitions #

  • analysis — distinguishing elements, rules, constraints, relations, or states;
  • synthesis — connecting elements into an interpretation, decision, or plan;
  • deduction, induction, and abduction — different forms of inference, not ready-made neural or Temporistics types.

A real task usually combines several operations.

Evidence map by domain #

Domain Laboratory tasks Frequently discussed systems Limit of inference
reasoning working memory, rules, relations, analogies lateral and rostrolateral PFC, parietal cortex, sometimes basal ganglia does not measure Psychosophy Logic as a whole
decision and effort comparing options, subjective value, effort, and conflict vmPFC, ventral striatum, dACC/dmPFC, anterior insula, and frontoparietal networks does not reduce “volition” to one network
emotion state discrimination, reappraisal, acceptance, expression distributed cortical-subcortical patterns including insula, amygdala, cingulate, and prefrontal cortex the amygdala is not an “emotion centre”
body and logistics interoception, multistep planning, navigation anterior insula and dACC; frontoparietal, hippocampal, retrosplenial, and motor systems does not locate Psychosophy Physics

Temporal experience and forms of inference #

Laboratory deduction often involves frontoparietal networks; rule discovery is studied as one analogue of induction. Causal reasoning relies on dynamic coordination between frontoparietal executive networks (rule-based deduction and constraint checking) and midline/hippocampal default networks (retrieving episodic precedents, mental time travel, and counterfactual simulation). Autobiographical memory and imagining future episodes share part of this core network. Abduction operates retrospectively by drawing on past memory traces to evaluate plausible causes of present outcomes.

These findings do not establish a neurobiology of Temporistics. A narrower hypothesis is that Temporistics scale results might be compared with behaviour in tasks such as:

Operation Example task What to observe
deduction apply a temporal “if–then” rule accuracy, time, explanation of the transition
induction find a pattern in a series of episodes examples needed, hypothesis changes, rule transfer
abduction / retrospective causality propose causes of an unexpected event or analyze past precedent range of alternatives, data requests, confidence revision, etiology depth
counterfactual simulation evaluate "what would have happened if X was different in the past" alternative branch plausibility, causal attribution precision
past–future describe a past episode and several future paths specificity, flexibility, distinction between fact and interpretation

Education, language, anxiety, experience, and task familiarity should be considered before testing any added association with a typological scale.

Analogies to BWB levels #

These are research directions, not a physiology of compatibility:

Level Possible research bridge Observable joint action Invalid shortcut
strategic / Temporistics memory, imagining future episodes, narrative build several scenarios and update direction “matching brain activity means a shared path”
operational / Psychosophy task control, value, effort, interoception distribute load and revisit a decision “a PFC or HRV measure means a good pair”
tactical / Socionics attention, language, mentalizing, turn-taking listen, clarify, and repair misunderstanding “inter-brain synchrony means understanding”

The value-moral foundation cannot be inferred from physiology. Sleep, pain, illness, hunger, stress, and material pressure may affect the processes discussed by all three typological submodels and often provide a simpler explanation.

Safe research sequence #

1. Define the operation and measure it behaviourally first.

2. Preregister alternative explanations and a falsification condition.

3. Compare any typological scale with context, skill, and load.

4. Use neuroimaging or physiology only as an additional layer in ethical, consent-based research.

5. Do not turn group correlations into a conclusion about one person or pair.

Observed behaviour, consent, safety, and the quality of a shared decision remain primary. See also operational-compatibility-en and neuroscience-compatibility-bridges-en.
