A Resource Reading of Afanasyev's Model

Status and scope #

This is a project interpretation of Alexander Afanasyev's Psychosophy for the operational level of Before We Build. It treats the ordering of Volition, Logic, Emotion, and Physics as a possible map of where a person more often sets a standard, supports a shared process, experiences tension, or accepts a ready-made frame.

Psychosophy is not treated here as a validated psychometric model. “Resource” is a metaphor for attention, confidence, priority, and readiness to act—not measurable energy or an established causal mechanism.

What is attributed to the source #

In Afanasyev's materials, the four aspects occupy four positions in a formula. The names and short descriptions below report that tradition; the resource reading on the right is BWB's interpretation and needs testing.

Position Attributed description Cautious BWB translation
1st result-oriented, confident, “hammer” possible tendency to set a standard independently and finish quickly
2nd process-oriented, flexible, “river” possible tendency to sustain dialogue and joint adjustment
3rd vulnerable, tense, “sore spot” possible sensitivity to evaluation and repeated checking
4th low-priority, receptive possible readiness to simplify, delegate, or accept an external frame

These descriptions do not mean more or less human ability. The same position may look different under illness, trauma, poverty, professional training, family role, or social pressure.

Four domains of action #

Aspect Question for observation, not a conclusion about a person
Volition (V) How are decisions made, boundaries voiced, and responsibility distributed?
Logic (L) How are claims checked, explanations built, and mistakes acknowledged?
Emotion (E) How is atmosphere created and emotional expression regulated?
Physics (F) How are body, health, money, possessions, rest, and household life organized?

A code such as ELVF only denotes an aspect order within this system. It is not an observable object, a diagnosis, or a compressed account of the whole person.

What can be observed #

A research hypothesis requires recurring episodes across different conditions:

  • where someone states a ready standard without prompting;
  • where they ask questions and help another person shape a decision;
  • where evaluation triggers disproportionate tension or repeated checking;
  • where they willingly accept another person's organization while retaining a right to object;
  • how the pattern changes with fatigue, power, risk, and role changes.

One behaviour is insufficient: enjoying debate does not establish a Logic position, expressiveness an Emotion position, modesty a Volition position, or illness and poverty a Physics position.

Possible pair mechanisms #

These are competing hypotheses, not compatibility rules:

Observed encounter Possible resource Possible tension What to check
one sets a standard and the other accepts the frame quick division of roles agreement may conceal loss of voice can either person safely refuse and reopen the decision?
one supports a process and the other checks anxiously learning and careful feedback help may become guardianship or dependency do autonomy and reciprocity increase?
both set a standard in one domain clarity and initiative struggle for control are there shared rules for resolving disagreement?
both give a domain little priority simplicity important duties remain unowned who notices consequences and assumes responsibility?

The same episodes may be explained by experience, culture, power, skill, safety, or current load. These alternatives should be tested before a typological account.

What cannot be inferred #

A Psychosophy code does not establish morality, dignity, faith, maturity, safety, occupation, diagnosis, readiness for marriage, or relationship outcome. A higher position is not better than a lower one; vulnerability does not excuse control, shaming, or avoiding responsibility. The model does not replace observed conduct, consent, or medical and professional help.

Practical questions #

  • Where does each of us usually lead, and where do we prefer support?
  • In which domain does help feel collaborative, and in which does it feel coercive?
  • Which decisions need explicit consent, assigned responsibility, and a review date?
  • What would change in our explanation if the typological hypothesis were wrong?

Research programme #

The testable questions are typing reproducibility, discriminability of the four proposed modes, stability across contexts, and added explanatory value after skills, roles, and life history are considered. Until then, the resource reading remains a way to formulate questions.
