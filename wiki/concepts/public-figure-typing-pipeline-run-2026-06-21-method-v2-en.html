Historical Methodology-v2 Review of Public Figures — 2026-06-21 #

Status #

This is a historical record of the second review, conducted before the current evidence protocol. It improved the initial table by explicitly considering source quality, role overfit, and alternatives, but still proposed codes from a corpus that was too thin. Candidates are retained to audit the method's development, not as current conclusions about people.

Ontology supersession notice #

The Socionics candidates in this record predate the BWB content–operation account of aspects and the separate account of position modes. No retrospective recalculation has been made. Any future reanalysis must gather evidence for both layers under socionics-reality-modeling-en rather than translating the old code mechanically.

Method changes in v2 #

  • observable traces were discussed before a four-letter code;
  • an official biography was treated as strong for facts and weak for personal pattern;
  • office, genre, theological school, and crisis were considered alternatives;
  • Temporistics required distinguishing a temporal topic from a proposed positional mode;
  • text analysis was treated as auxiliary rather than decisive.

The current process goes further: people decide after blind coding, while AI may only summarize, organize material, and suggest questions.

Observations and competing explanations #

  • Sannikov's theological and historical systematization may reflect professional specialization.
  • Romaniuk's concern for maturity and structural teaching are both demanded by pastoral work.
  • Antoniuk and other union leaders receive organization and responsibility from office.
  • Bandura's future-generation theme may be an assignment of the source genre.
  • For Mokhnenko, Vecherkovskyi, and other ministers, crisis or mission foregrounds action, risk, and future direction.
  • Christian speech about meaning and eternity is not a Temporistics marker.

Next evidence #

Full materials across genres and years, spontaneous answers, exact excerpts, episodes of error and revision, and independent de-identified coding are needed. If role and context explain a trace equally well, no code is selected.

Non-inferences #

The record does not evaluate faith, character, morality, safety, pastoral fitness, authority, or future behaviour. It is not used for staffing, reputation, marriage, or trust decisions. See public-figure-typing-evidence-protocol-en and public-figure-typing-methodology-audit-en.
