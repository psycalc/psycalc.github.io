Afanasyev's Psychosophy Model

Afanasyev's Psychosophy Model #

English · Русский · Українська

Definition #

Psychosophy, also called Psyche-Yoga in some traditions, arranges four named aspects—Will, Logic, Emotion, and Physics—into four positions. A type code is a compact reference to that permutation. It is not a measurement of the whole person or four independent strength scores.

Four aspects #

Aspect Domain named by the school Boundary
Will agency, priority, decision, assertion not moral worth or leadership fitness
Logic reasoning, argument, explanation not intelligence
Emotion emotional expression and tone not kindness or Socionics Ethics
Physics bodily, material, and practical concerns not health, wealth, or appearance

These are model domains. Their usefulness and boundaries require empirical testing.

Code structure #

ELVF means 1E–2L–3V–4F; LEVF means 1L–2E–3V–4F. Each aspect appears exactly once. The code therefore records order by position, not four numeric ratings. Localized letters may differ while referring to the same positions.

Claims about positions #

Psychosophy schools commonly describe the first and fourth positions as result-oriented and the second and third as process-oriented; they also attach confidence, flexibility, vulnerability, or low priority to positions. These descriptions are attributed school claims, not established traits. Terms such as “excessive,” “weak,” or “inadequate” are especially liable to be mistaken for diagnosis or human value.

Before We Build interpretation #

BWB studies Psychosophy at the operational level as a heuristic model of proposed latent processes of synthesis, analysis, and action organization. This mapping may generate questions about initiative, division of responsibility, support, revision, and pressure. It does not establish the mechanism or predict a pair’s outcome.

Observation and inference #

“The person proposed two task divisions and revised one after feedback” can be observed. “This is second-position Logic” is an interpretation. A fair test needs predefined indicators, multiple contexts, rival explanations, independent ratings, and evidence that the model adds value beyond skill, role, culture, stress, and incentives.

What cannot be concluded #

A Psychosophy code cannot establish dignity, morality, faith, intelligence, diagnosis, profession, consent, safety, compatibility, or destiny. Past, Present, Future, and Eternity belong to Temporistics, a separate model.
