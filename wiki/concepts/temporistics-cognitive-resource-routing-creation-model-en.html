Temporistics as a Cognitive-Resource Routing Hypothesis

Temporistics as a Cognitive-Resource Routing Hypothesis #

Status and boundaries #

This is a BWB research hypothesis, not an established neuroscientific, psychometric, or theological model. It proposes reading Temporistics descriptions as possible differences in the priority given to temporal tasks: engaging with the present, interpreting the past, constructing future paths, and forming meaning beyond the immediate situation.

“Resource” means attention, working memory, time, and effort. It is not physical energy, a measured share of the brain, or a numerical weight. Neuroscience concepts serve only as analogies for future task design.

Four temporal coordinates #

Aspect Question Thinking process Possible observable task
Present “What is happening, and what is my place now?” situational-experiential reasoning notice current conditions, role, resources, and need for action in the moment
Past “Why did this happen, and what led to it?” causal reasoning (causal analysis) distinguish facts from interpretations, identify chronology, causes, and precedents
Future “Which paths are possible, and what would change the course?” scenario-probabilistic reasoning construct probabilistic scenarios, evaluate risks, and set review criteria
Eternity “Why is this, and which meaning outlasts this situation?” meaning-making and sense-making (teleology) formulate overarching value frames, orientations, and test their application

In BWB, Temporistics belongs to the strategic level: it concerns organization of temporal and existential direction, not moral values. Distinguishing the causal reasoning of the Past (“Why?”) from the meaning-making of Eternity (“To what end / Why is this significant?”) prevents conflating historical causality with overarching meaning.

Hypothesis about positional modes #

The following table is BWB's interpretation, not a canonical definition of the system:

Position Proposed mode What might be observed Competing explanation
1st independent frame setting (predominantly top-down deductive movement) a person quickly formulates an orientation/meaning and applies it to specifics experience, formal authority, occupational role
2nd dialogical refinement and comparison a person helps a group compare histories, scenarios, and meaning systems facilitation skill, agreeableness
3rd tense search for an explanation (inductive/abductive signal gathering) a person keenly seeks hypotheses, notices contradictions in causes or meaning, delays closure anxiety, trauma, situational uncertainty
4th accepting or delegating an external frame a person readily uses an existing route from tradition/partner while remaining able to revise it trust, lack of knowledge, low topic importance

Deduction, induction, and abduction may name operations within a task. They are not established entities of the positions and should not be moved into a canonical registry.

Biopsychological motivational parallels (Cloninger, Gray) #

As a heuristic model of cognitive resource allocation, the four-position structure shares structural analogies with basic systems of behavioral motivation (Cloninger's TCI and Gray/McNaughton's RST):

Position Motivational-regulatory mode Biopsychological analogy Manifestation in aspect domain
1st Autonomous expansion, initiative, appetitive drive Novelty Seeking (NS) / BAS (Behavioral Approach) Exploratory activity and autonomous mastery of one's domain (1N — immediate environment of the moment; 1F — new trajectories and goals; 1P — reconstruction of origins; 1E — assertion of overarching meaning).
2nd Dialogue, plasticity, process orientation Reward Dependence (RD) / Social plasticity Flexible cooperation, exchanging alternatives, sensitivity to feedback.
3rd Risk monitoring, hypervigilance, doubt Harm Avoidance (HA) / BIS (Behavioral Inhibition) Hypersensitivity to errors, competence anxiety, doubts regarding the right to judge, and behavioral inhibition.
4th Resource conservation, delegation Low reactivity / Resource conservation Minimizing attentional costs, lack of internal struggle, peaceful adoption of an external frame.

These correspondences serve solely as analogies for experimental design. BWB categorically rejects reducing positions to monoamines (dopamine, serotonin): dispositional temperament traits and situational cognitive frames operate at distinct levels of description.

Community as a context for distributed attention #

In a family, team, or community, different people may support different temporal tasks at a given moment: preserving relevant memory, seeing current constraints, developing future options, or recalling meaning. This is a situational role, not an assignment conferred by type.

A possible resource of complementarity must be distinguished from unsafe dependency. Check whether roles can change, whether each person has a voice, whether a frame can be challenged, and whether “distribution” conceals control or avoidance of responsibility.

Christian application reading #

A Christian application may interpret diverse ways of remembering, acting, planning, and seeking meaning through creation and the image of “one body, many members” (1 Corinthians 12:13–22). This is the application's normative frame, not evidence for Temporistics.

Type does not establish vocation, spiritual gift, maturity, or church role; it does not excuse sin, passivity, or control; and it does not replace Scripture, conscience, repentance, or mature counsel.

How to test it #

Research should compare the typological hypothesis with simpler explanations: age, education, language, anxiety, life experience, role, stress, and task familiarity. Useful measures include the number of scenarios considered, accuracy of event recall, willingness to update a conclusion, and ability to separate fact from interpretation.

The hypothesis weakens if independent raters do not agree, predicted differences fail to replicate, or context explains behaviour better.

What cannot be inferred #

A profile does not establish human worth, brain type, morality, safety, destiny, vocation, suitability for marriage, or relationship outcome. This page offers no numerical budget, coefficient, or verdict for a pair.
